# Aman Desai

I study how machine learning can help people design and understand hardware. My
interests are in computer architecture, EDA, and agentic methods that learn from
simulation and timing feedback.

I am an Undergraduate Researcher at [UCSB ArchLab](https://www.arch.cs.ucsb.edu),
advised by [Jonathan Balkind](https://jbalkind.github.io/), and I study Computer Science
in the College of Creative Studies at UC Santa Barbara. I also work with
[Zishen Wan](https://zishenwan.github.io/) at Columbia University on workload synthesis
for power characterization. Over the past year I was a research engineer at
[ChipAgents](https://chipagents.ai). I am currently applying to PhD programs.
